Privacy Policy for Brick Breaker: Shoot & Blast Rewards

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Brick Breaker ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Brick Breaker: Shoot & Blast Rewards app ("the app").

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address, a player name and a password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are not visible to us.

Game data. We store your level, stars, gold bars, daily activity (Gold Map level reached today, free boosters used today), Classic Map level, boosters in your bag, hearts and settings so your progress is saved.

Withdrawal information. If you request a withdrawal, we collect the UPI ID you enter, the gold bar amount, the rupee amount and the request date.

Advertising and device data. The app shows interstitial ads (after finished levels), a banner ad on the level-complete screen and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Watching an ad never gives gold bars or cash; an optional rewarded ad only gives game help such as a free booster, an extra ball or a heart. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and save your progress.
  • To give rewards and to review and process withdrawal requests.
  • To show ads and to prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To maintain and improve the app.

3. Third-party services

The app uses services provided by Google: Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) and Google AdMob. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • AdMob: support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers described above, and where required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open Profile, then Delete Account. This removes your login and your game data. Withdrawal requests that were already submitted are kept for payment and accounting records. You can also ask for deletion by emailing us at the address below from your registered email.

6. Security

We use Google Firebase security features to protect your data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Rewards

Gold bars are earned only by playing and winning levels (in the Gold Map, up to 50 levels a day, up to ₹1 a day). The Classic Map is for fun and gives no gold bars. Gold bars have no cash value until a withdrawal request is reviewed and approved. We aim to pay approved requests within 24 hours. We may refuse or reverse rewards obtained by cheating, automation, multiple accounts or any other misuse of the app.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact us

For questions, or to request account deletion, contact: coinforgegames.support@gmail.com
